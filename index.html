Three tools, one app

Pic2Report isn't just templates

Underneath, one capture-and-enhance engine. On top, three purpose-built tools that each solve a different job.

Pillar 01

Document Scanner

Capture or import photos and turn them straight into a clean, document-quality PDF — cropped, enhanced, ready to file. Not tied to any template, just the fastest path from photo to PDF.

Scenario — a stack of paper receipts becomes one crisp black & white PDF for your expense folder, in under a minute.

Pillar 02

Report Builder

The same capture-and-enhance pipeline, fed into a purpose-built template — so the output is a structured, formatted report instead of a raw scan.

Scenario — an inspector photographs six issues on a job site and gets a formatted checklist report with photo evidence attached to each item.

Pillar 03

PDF Merge

Combine multiple existing PDFs and/or images into a single PDF — independent of the scanner and the templates.

Scenario — a contractor stitches together five separate site-photo PDFs and a signed estimate into one packet to email the client.
