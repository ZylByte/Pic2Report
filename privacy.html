2. Information the App Accesses

Pic2Report may access information on your device when you explicitly use certain features.

Photos and Images

When you select images from your device or use the camera, Pic2Report accesses those images only to provide features such as:

  • Creating PDF reports
  • Scanning documents
  • Cropping and rotating images
  • Applying image enhancements
  • Creating multi-page PDF documents

Images selected by you are processed locally on your device.

Camera

The App may request camera access when you choose to capture an image for document scanning or report creation.

Camera access is used only when you actively use the camera-related functionality.

Files and Documents

When you select PDF or image files for features such as PDF merging or document scanning, Pic2Report accesses the files you explicitly select.

The App does not automatically access unrelated files on your device.

3. Local and Offline Processing

Pic2Report is designed primarily as an offline application.

Your photos, PDFs, report content, drafts, templates, and generated documents are processed and stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export a file using another application or service.

Pic2Report does not require an account or user registration.

The App does not require users to upload their documents, photos, or generated reports to Pic2Report servers.

Pic2Report does not intentionally collect or transmit your personal documents, photos, report content, drafts, or generated PDF files to Pic2Report servers.

Pic2Report stores files and application data locally on your device as necessary to provide its functionality.

This may include:

These files remain on your device until you delete them through the App, your device's file management tools, or by uninstalling the App, subject to the operating system's behavior.

Pic2Report does not maintain a remote copy of these files.

Pic2Report is designed to process user documents and images locally whenever possible.
